# Review

## Summary
The paper presents a framework that integrates symbolic equivalence into symbolic regression through equality graphs (e-graphs) to accelerate the discovery of optimal expressions. The paper shows that the proposed approach can be used in combination with Monte Carlo Tree Search, Deep Reinforcement Learning, and Large Language Models. The authors present theoretical analysis for their approach and experimental results on several datasets.

## Strengths
- The paper addresses a relevant problem in the field of symbolic regression, which is the exponential growth of the search space of expression.

- The authors propose a unified framework that can be used with different approaches, such as Monte Carlo Tree Search, Deep Reinforcement Learning, and Large Language Models.

- The authors provide a theoretical analysis of their approach.

## Weaknesses
- The authors should provide more details on how the e-graphs are constructed and updated. This is an important part of the proposed approach, and more information would help readers understand the method better.

- The authors should discuss the scalability of their approach. How does the proposed method perform with larger datasets or more complex expressions?

- The authors should provide more details on the experimental setup, such as the hyperparameters used for the experiments and the implementation details of the proposed approach.
