# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces a new dataset, VIBEFACE, which contains images and videos of 50 individuals for face recognition and verification applications. The dataset contains four types of scenarios: standardized photos, selfie photos, selfie videos, and verification videos. The authors evaluated the dataset on two tasks: face detection and face verification. Three face detection methods and two face verification methods are evaluated on the dataset.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The dataset contains various scenarios and environments, which is beneficial for research on robust face recognition and face verification.
3. The authors evaluated the dataset on two important tasks: face detection and face verification, which can benefit the research community.

## Weaknesses
1. The scale of the dataset is too small. It contains images and videos of only 50 individuals, which is too small for training, validating, and testing face recognition and face verification models. The dataset will be useful only for fine-tuning or zero-shot evaluation.

2. The evaluation of face verification is not comprehensive. The authors only evaluated two methods, which is not enough. Additionally, the authors did not compare the performance on the proposed dataset with that on other datasets. It is important to show the generalization ability of the methods on different datasets.

3. The authors did not provide any insights or conclusions from the evaluation results. It is better to provide some insights or suggestions based on the evaluation results.
