# Review

## Summary
This paper presents MOCHA, a curated resource for developing and evaluating multi-sample SRT methods. MOCHA integrates molecular profiles, spatial profiles, and high-resolution H&E images across multiple subjects, with each sample paired with domain annotations from expert pathologists. For algorithm development and evaluation, MOCHA provides standardized data organization, efficient storage formats for large-scale processing, and protocols for handling batch effects in multi-sample integration.

## Weaknesses
1. This paper appears to be a data curation work, primarily integrating existing datasets. The authors should clarify the technical innovations or methodological developments that make this resource unique. If the contribution is mainly engineering-based, it may be more suitable for a data in Briefs article rather than a full ICLR paper.
2. The authors should provide a detailed comparison with existing spatial transcriptomics (ST) databases, discussing how MOCHA offers novel capabilities or improvements over existing resources. 
3. The authors should describe the data access and usage policies for MOCHA, including any restrictions or requirements for researchers who wish to use the dataset.
