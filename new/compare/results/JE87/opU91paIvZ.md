# Review

## Summary
The paper studies the problem of controlling the chain-of-thought (CoT) generated by language models (LMs). In particular, the authors propose a method to improve the faithfulness and conciseness of CoT. The method involves using another LM to transform the initial CoT into a more faithful or concise one, and then use the transformed CoT for training the original LM. The authors conduct experiments on MMLU-Pro, GSM8K, and MATH500, and demonstrate that the proposed method can improve the faithfulness and conciseness of CoT without degrading task performance.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The problem of controlling the chain-of-thought (CoT) generated by language models (LMs) is important and well-motivated.
3. The proposed method is simple and intuitive.

## Weaknesses
1. The authors propose to use a prior policy $\pi_s$ to transform the initial CoT $z$ into a more monitorable $z_s$. However, the authors do not provide a detailed discussion on how to obtain such a prior policy. In the experiments, the authors use a Qwen 2.5-7B Instruct model as the prior policy, which is significantly larger than the DeepSeek R1 Qwen-1.5B base model used for training. The use of a much larger model as the prior policy raises concerns about the scalability and practicality of the proposed method.
2. The authors claim that the proposed method can improve the faithfulness and conciseness of CoT without degrading task performance. However, the authors do not provide a detailed discussion on why the proposed method does not degrade task performance. In particular, it is not clear why the use of a prior policy does not lead to a degradation in task performance. A more detailed analysis and discussion on this aspect would strengthen the paper.
3. The authors propose to use a prior policy $\pi_s$ to transform the initial CoT $z$ into a more monitorable $z_s$. However, the authors do not provide a detailed discussion on the potential limitations and challenges of the proposed method. In particular, it is not clear how to ensure that the transformed CoT $z_s$ is still semantically equivalent to the initial CoT $z$. A more detailed analysis and discussion on this aspect would strengthen the paper.

## Questions
1. How to obtain a prior policy $\pi_s$? How does the quality of the prior policy affect the performance of the proposed method?
2. Why does the proposed method not degrade task performance? How does the proposed method ensure the semantic equivalence between the transformed CoT $z_s$ and the initial CoT $z$?
