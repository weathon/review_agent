# Review

## Summary
The paper introduces LPFQA, a new benchmark designed to evaluate Large Language Models (LLMs) on complex, specialized knowledge tasks derived from professional forums across 20 fields. LPFQA addresses limitations in existing benchmarks by focusing on long-tail knowledge and real-world applications. It introduces four key innovations: fine-grained evaluation dimensions, a hierarchical difficulty structure, authentic professional scenario modeling, and interdisciplinary knowledge integration. The authors evaluated 12 mainstream LLMs on LPFQA, revealing significant performance disparities, especially in specialized reasoning tasks. The paper claims that LPFQA provides a robust, authentic, and discriminative benchmark for advancing LLM evaluation and guiding future model development.

## Strengths
- The paper proposes a new benchmark, LPFQA, which is designed to address the limitations of existing benchmarks by focusing on long-tail knowledge and real-world applications. 
- The paper evaluates 12 mainstream LLMs on LPFQA, providing a comprehensive view of their performance on complex, specialized knowledge tasks. 
- The paper provides a detailed analysis of the performance of the evaluated models, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses across different fields.

## Weaknesses
- The paper does not provide a clear comparison with existing benchmarks, making it difficult to assess the novelty and effectiveness of LPFQA. 
- The paper lacks a detailed discussion of the limitations of LPFQA, which would provide a more balanced view of the benchmark's capabilities and potential drawbacks.

## Questions
- What are the limitations of LPFQA, and how might they impact the evaluation of LLMs?
- How does LPFQA compare to existing benchmarks in terms of size, scope, and difficulty?
- What are the potential ethical considerations associated with using professional forum data for benchmark construction?
