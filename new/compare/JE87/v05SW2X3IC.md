# Review

## Summary
This paper introduces a learnable Gray-Wyner Network to disentangle shared information from task-specific details for multiple vision tasks. The proposed method is theoretically justified and achieves good performance in the experiments.

## Strengths
1. The paper is well-written and easy to follow.
2. The paper provides a theoretical analysis of the proposed method.
3. The paper achieves good performance in the experiments.

## Weaknesses
1. The paper targets at three channels: a common channel and two private channels. However, the experiments only involve two channels (common and private). It would be better to provide experiments with three channels.
2. The paper focuses on the Gray-Wyner Network, which is a simple network. It would be better to discuss more complex networks, e.g., the network in [1].
3. The paper only provides the performance of the proposed method. It would be better to provide a comparison with existing methods, e.g., [1].

[1] Lossy Compression for Lossless Prediction. NeurIPS 2021.
