# Review

## Summary
This paper studies the polyhedral complex formed by the activation patterns of a ReLU network. More specifically, the authors study the connectivity of the complex, where each polytope is a face of a neuron's activation region. The main results are upper and lower bounds on the average connectivity of the complex, as well as upper and lower bounds on the connectivity graph diameter. The results are proven theoretically and validated empirically.

## Strengths
This is a very well written paper, and I enjoyed reading it. The proofs are easy to follow, and the empirical results validate the theoretical findings. The results are novel, and I think they are significant for the community.

## Weaknesses
I do not see any major weakness. 

I have a few minor suggestions. 

1. The authors may want to add a few sentences discussing the limitations of the work.
2. The authors may want to add a paragraph discussing the implications of the results. For example, how can the results be used in other works? Do the results have any impact on the training or design of neural networks?
3. The authors may want to move some of the empirical results to the main text. For example, the histograms in Figures 6 and 7 are very interesting.
