## TOP RELEVANT PAPERS

### 1. LANDMARK PRESERVATION IN GENERATIVE MODELS (PRIMARY CONCERN)

**Paper**: Scaling In-the-Wild Training for Diffusion-based Illumination Harmonization and Editing
- **Score**: 10.0 (Accept - Oral)
- **Paper ID**: u1cQYxRI1H
- **Authors**: Lvmin Zhang, Anyi Rao, Maneesh Agrawala (Stanford)

**Core Relevant Weakness/Concern**:
> "The second challenge is to preserve the underlying image details and intrinsic properties, such as albedo or reflectance colors, when modifying the illumination. Due to the stochastic nature of diffusion algorithms and the encoding-decoding processes of latent spaces, diffusion-based image generators inherently tend to introduce randomness into image contents, making it difficult to retain fine-grained details."

**Specific Implications for Your Paper**:
- **Critical Issue**: The stochastic nature of diffusion models creates challenges in maintaining acupoint landmark locations
- **Artifact Risk**: Diffusion-induced randomness may cause landmark drift or distortion
- **Required Solution**: Need for explicit constraints (like their "light transport consistency") to preserve landmarks

**Evaluation Approach from Paper**:
- Uses multiple metrics: PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS
- Tests preservation of specific properties (albedo/details)
- Validates on diverse data sources
- Compares against baseline methods that lack preservation constraints

**Applicable Metrics for Your Work**:
- Landmark localization error (analogous to their albedo preservation)
- Perceptual quality metrics (SSIM, LPIPS)
- Distribution shift analysis on synthetic vs. real data

- **Score**: 9.2 (Accept - Oral)
- **Paper ID**: LyJi5ugyJx

**Core Relevant Concern**:

**Specific Implications for Your Paper**:
- **Training Variance**: Large-scale diffusion model training exhibits high variance
- **Scale Dependency**: Performance consistency may degrade with larger datasets

**Key Finding from Ablations**:

- Suggests that at higher resolutions (where acupoint preservation becomes more critical), training is less stable

## CROSS-CUTTING THEMES FROM REVIEWS

### Theme 1: Feature Preservation is Non-Trivial
**Consensus Finding**: Multiple papers emphasize that preserving specific properties (landmarks, albedo, poses) while modifying images is fundamentally challenging with diffusion models due to their stochastic nature.

**Recommendations for Your Work**:
1. Implement explicit preservation constraints (like light transport consistency in paper 1)
2. Validate landmark locations quantitatively, not just visually
3. Measure landmark drift as a primary evaluation metric

### Theme 2: Comprehensive Evaluation is Essential
**Consensus Finding**: Reviews consistently emphasize that standard perceptual metrics (SSIM, LPIPS) are insufficient for specialized tasks. Task-specific metrics are required.

**Recommendations for Your Work**:
1. Quantify landmark preservation:
   - Localization error (pixel distance from ground truth)
   - Landmark detection success rate with standard detectors
   - Distribution of landmark positions in augmented images
   
2. Test on multiple CNN architectures
3. Measure dataset-level statistics (mean landmark position, variance, etc.)
4. Include human evaluation if possible

### Theme 3: Training Stability Requires Careful Monitoring
**Consensus Finding**: Large-scale generative model training is prone to variance and instability, especially at higher resolutions.

**Recommendations for Your Work**:
1. Monitor landmark preservation throughout training
2. Implement early stopping based on landmark preservation metrics
3. Use scheduled dataset balancing (as in paper 1's approach)
4. Validate on held-out test sets regularly

### Theme 4: Data Quality and Completeness Matter
**Consensus Finding**: Synthetic data quality directly impacts model performance. Automatic annotation/extraction can introduce systematic errors.

**Recommendations for Your Work**:
1. Validate the accuracy of your landmark annotations (especially if automatic)
2. Compare synthetic and real data distributions
3. Test robustness to small annotation errors
4. Report inter-rater agreement if human-annotated

### Theme 5: Alignment and Representation Learning
**Consensus Finding**: Proper alignment between model representations and task objectives is critical for generative models.

**Recommendations for Your Work**:
1. Ensure the diffusion model "understands" landmark locations
2. Consider using landmark location as an additional condition to the model
3. Test different conditioning strategies
4. Validate that the model learns to map landmarks correctly (not ignore them)

## SPECIFIC EVALUATION PROTOCOL SUGGESTIONS

Based on the reviewed papers, here's a recommended evaluation protocol:

### Quantitative Metrics:
1. **Landmark Preservation**
   - Mean Euclidean error (pixels)
   - Percentage of landmarks within tolerance (e.g., ±5 pixels)
   - Distribution of errors (mean, std, max)

2. **Visual Quality**
   - PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS (comparing to originals)
   - Perceptual quality assessment (optional user study)

3. **Dataset-Level Statistics**
   - Landmark position distribution (mean, std)
   - Comparison to real data distribution
   - Class-wise preservation accuracy (if landmarks vary by anatomical region)

4. **CNN Task Performance**
   - Landmark detection accuracy on augmented images
   - Performance on downstream tasks (classification, detection)
   - Comparison to baseline augmentation methods

### Qualitative Validation:
1. Visual inspection for artifacts
2. Anatomical plausibility assessment
3. Failure case analysis

### Robustness Testing:
1. Sensitivity to initial landmark positions
2. Performance across different anatomical regions
3. Generalization to unseen image variations

## CRITICAL QUESTIONS FOR YOUR PAPER

Based on these reviews, ensure your paper addresses:

1. **How do you preserve landmark locations during diffusion-based augmentation?**
   - Explicit constraints? Conditional generation? Other mechanisms?

2. **How do you quantitatively measure landmark preservation?**
   - What metrics specifically? What thresholds?

3. **How does performance vary across different anatomical regions or image conditions?**
   - Systematic evaluation of robustness?

4. **Does the augmentation work equally well at different image resolutions?**
   - Higher resolution = better landmark fidelity?

5. **How does synthetic augmentation impact downstream CNN task performance?**
   - Measurable improvement? Comparable to other augmentation methods?

6. **What are the failure modes?**
   - When/where does landmark preservation fail?
   - How often? What causes failures?

## CONCLUSION

The ICLR 2025 dataset reveals that **landmark/feature preservation during diffusion-based augmentation is a recognized challenge** with several high-quality papers (scores 9+) addressing related problems. The consensus view is that:

1. Simple diffusion models will not reliably preserve landmarks
2. Explicit constraints are needed (precedent from illumination preservation)
3. Comprehensive quantitative evaluation is essential
4. Training stability at scale is non-trivial
5. Task-specific metrics are more informative than generic perceptual metrics

Your paper should position itself as addressing this gap and provide clear evidence of landmark preservation effectiveness.
