THE 7 WEAKNESSES (Ranked by Severity)
================================================================================

HIGH SEVERITY (4 weaknesses):
────────────────────────────

1. LACK OF EXPLICIT LANDMARK PRESERVATION CONSTRAINT
   Problem: Relies on implicit landmark preservation; no explicit geometric constraints
   Quote: "Due to stochastic nature of diffusion algorithms... making it difficult to
           retain fine-grained details... requires careful design of training objectives
           and constraints." (IC-Light, p. 2)
   Fix: Add geometric loss, anchor-point masking, or optical flow consistency to preserve
        acupoint positions

2. INADEQUATE QUALITY FILTERING FOR ANATOMICAL ACCURACY
   Problem: CLIP score filtering catches global failures but misses local anatomical errors
   Quote: "primary goal is not to perform precise quality ranking, but rather to eliminate
           obviously mismatched samples... sometimes fail to meet specific dataset
           requirements." (KnowData, p. 4-5)
   Fix: Multi-stage filtering with landmark detector validation and anatomical checks

4. MISSING PER-LANDMARK ACCURACY METRICS
   Problem: Standard metrics (accuracy, F1) hide whether specific acupoints are preserved
   Quote: "We conducted quantitative comparisons using metrics such as PSNR, SSIM, and
           LPIPS... retains fine-grained image details and intrinsic properties."
           (IC-Light, p. 7-9)
   Fix: Report per-acupoint localization error, anatomical consistency, landmark detection
        confidence for all 14+ acupoints

7. NO REALISTIC DOWNSTREAM CLINICAL VALIDATION
   Problem: Academic benchmarks don't guarantee real clinical utility
   Quote: "We provide pretrained models to facilitate applications across diverse
           domains... handle more in-the-wild scenarios." (IC-Light, p. 2)
   Fix: Test on real clinical data from different settings; validate with practitioners;
        measure generalization across skin tones, face types, and clinical conditions

MEDIUM SEVERITY (3 weaknesses):
───────────────────────────────

3. UNCONTROLLED DATA DISTRIBUTION DIVERSITY
   Problem: No explicit control over facial pose/expression/ethnicity variation in synthetic data
   Quote: "Without well-suited regularization and constraints, the model can easily degrade
           to random behaviors... arbitrary changes driven by dataset local minima."
           (IC-Light, p. 5)
   Fix: Conduct distribution analysis; control synthesis parameters; verify augmented data
        complements real data

5. UNVALIDATED COMPONENT INTERACTIONS
   Problem: Combines three components (SD1.5, IP-Adapter, IC-Light) without proving they
            work together for landmark preservation
   Quote: "Existing studies employ various generation tricks but fail to effectively
           incorporate the relevant knowledge of the class itself into the generation
           process." (KnowData, p. 2-3)
   Fix: Comprehensive ablation studies isolating each component's impact on landmarks

6. NO PRINCIPLED DATA RATIO OPTIMIZATION
   Problem: Optimal mix of synthetic to real data is unknown and task-dependent
   Quote: "The ratio of offline to online data can significantly impact performance in
           different environments. Determining the optimal ratio for three types of data
           remains an open challenge." (CFDG, p. 10)
   Fix: Run experiments varying synthetic:real ratios (1:1, 1:2, 1:5, 1:10) and provide
        optimization guidance

KEY INSIGHT
================================================================================

The most important lesson from these papers is:

    "IMPLICIT CONSTRAINTS ARE INSUFFICIENT FOR TASKS REQUIRING
     FINE-GRAINED SPATIAL ACCURACY"

Evidence:
- IC-Light shows that vanilla diffusion without light transport constraints produces
  "random behaviors" and "color mismatch, incorrect details"
- IC-Light proves by ablation that removing explicit constraints causes significant quality
  degradation
- KnowData shows single-metric filtering misses domain-specific failures

Application to Acupoint Work:
- Relying on IP-Adapter's implicit identity preservation is insufficient for anatomical
  landmarks
- CLIP-score filtering alone cannot detect medical-specific acupoint errors
- Explicit landmark preservation constraints (geometric loss, anatomical bounds checking,
  landmark detection guidance) are necessary

This is not a minor point—it's the core difference between IC-Light's success and what
would be a failed approach using vanilla diffusion without constraints.
