KEY FINDINGS (6 PAPERS, RANKED BY RELEVANCE & QUALITY)
===============================================================================================

1. LANDMARK PRESERVATION CHALLENGE (MOST RELEVANT)
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Paper: "Scaling In-the-Wild Training for Diffusion-based Illumination Harmonization"
   Score: 10.0 (Accept - Oral)
   Paper ID: u1cQYxRI1H
   
   PRIMARY CONCERN:
   Diffusion models inherently introduce randomness that makes it difficult to retain
   fine-grained details. The stochastic nature prevents reliable preservation of 
   specific image properties.
   
   SOLUTION APPROACH:
   Implement explicit constraints (physical-based or task-based) during training to
   enforce preservation of target properties (analogous to their "light transport 
   consistency" constraint).
   
   EVALUATION APPROACH:
   - PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS metrics
   - Direct measurement of specific property preservation
   - Comparison against baselines without preservation constraints
   
   DIRECTLY APPLICABLE TO YOUR PAPER:
   HIGH - This paper solves an isomorphic problem (preserving illumination properties
   while modifying appearance). Your landmark preservation challenge can benefit from
   their constraint-based approach.


2. TRAINING STABILITY & SCALABILITY
   ────────────────────────────────
   Paper: "Simplifying, Stabilizing and Scaling Continuous-time Consistency Models"
   Score: 9.2 (Accept - Oral)
   Paper ID: LyJi5ugyJx
   
   KEY CONCERN:
   Training variance increases with model scale. Performance at high resolutions
   (512x512+) shows instability compared to lower resolutions.
   
   IMPLICATION FOR YOUR WORK:
   Landmark preservation quality may degrade as model scale increases. Higher
   resolutions needed for fine-grained landmarks may suffer from training instability.
   
   RECOMMENDATION:
   Monitor landmark preservation metrics throughout training; implement early stopping
   based on preservation quality, not just overall loss.


3. REPRESENTATION ALIGNMENT IN GENERATIVE MODELS
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────
   Paper: "Representation Alignment for Generation: Training Diffusion Transformers"
   Score: 9.0 (Accept - Oral)
   Paper ID: DJSZGGZYVi
   
   KEY CONCERN:
   Gaps between diffusion model internal representations and task objectives require
   explicit alignment during training.
   
   IMPLICATION FOR YOUR WORK:
   Model may not "understand" landmark locations without explicit representation
   alignment. Consider using representation quality metrics (CKA) to validate that
   model learns proper landmark mappings.


4. STRUCTURAL CONSISTENCY IN SYNTHETIC GENERATION
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────
   Paper: "TANGO: Co-Speech Gesture Video Reenactment with Diffusion Interpolation"
   Score: 8.5 (Accept - Oral)
   Paper ID: LbEWwJOufy
   
   KEY CONCERN:
   Automatic annotation/extraction of structural elements can introduce systematic
   errors. Data quality challenges affect downstream task performance.
   
   IMPLICATION FOR YOUR WORK:
   If using automatic landmark extraction, validate annotation accuracy. Measure
   robustness to small annotation errors. Test data quality impact on CNN performance.


5. COMPREHENSIVE EVALUATION METHODOLOGY
   ───────────────────────────────────
   Paper: "Scaling and evaluating sparse autoencoders"
   Score: 8.2 (Accept - Oral)
   Paper ID: tcsZt9ZNKD
   
   KEY FINDING:
   Standard perceptual metrics insufficient for specialized tasks. Task-specific
   metrics required for proper evaluation.
   
   RECOMMENDATION:
   Move beyond SSIM/LPIPS. Include landmark-specific metrics:
   - Localization error
   - Detection accuracy
   - Distribution preservation
   - Cross-architecture robustness


6. EFFICIENCY VS. QUALITY IN HIGH-RESOLUTION GENERATION
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Paper: "SANA: Efficient High-Resolution Text-to-Image Synthesis"
   Score: 8.5 (Accept - Oral)
   Paper ID: N8Oj1XhtYZ
   
   KEY CONSIDERATION:
   High-resolution generation requires more model capacity. Fine-grained landmark
   preservation creates computational/efficiency trade-offs.
   
   IMPLICATION FOR YOUR WORK:
   Balance augmentation quality with practical training costs. Consider resolution
   impact on both landmark preservation and model efficiency.

RECOMMENDED EVALUATION PROTOCOL
===============================================================================================

QUANTITATIVE METRICS:
1. Landmark Preservation:
   - Mean Euclidean error (pixels)
   - Percentage landmarks within tolerance (±N pixels)
   - Per-region accuracy
   - Detection rate on augmented images

2. Visual Quality:
   - PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS vs. originals
   - User study for artifact assessment

3. Dataset-Level:
   - Landmark position distribution (mean, std)
   - Real vs. synthetic distribution comparison
   - Feature-landmark correlation preservation

4. Task Performance:
   - CNN landmark detection on augmented data
   - Classification performance improvement
   - Comparison to baseline augmentation methods

QUALITATIVE VALIDATION:
- Visual inspection for artifacts and anatomical implausibility
- Failure case analysis
- Robustness across image conditions and anatomical regions
