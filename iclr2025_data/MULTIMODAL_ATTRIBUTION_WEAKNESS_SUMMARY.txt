================================================================================
WEAKNESS EXTRACTION SUMMARY: MULTIMODAL ATTRIBUTION METHODS
Papers Analyzed: 3 (SynthCLIP, ProtoDis-TBPS, ScImage)
================================================================================

CRITICAL WEAKNESSES FOUND

1. MULTIMODAL ALIGNMENT AND COHERENCE
   - Poor text-image alignment undermines attribution learning (SynthCLIP)
   - Content generation mismatches between modalities (SynthCLIP)
   - Modality gap prevents precise cross-modal attribution (ProtoDis-TBPS)
   STATUS: HIGH IMPACT - Core to multimodal attribution functionality

2. DISTRIBUTION SHIFT AND GENERALIZATION
   - Significant gap between synthetic training and real test data (SynthCLIP)
   - Synthetic data shows persistent underperformance on robustness datasets (SynthCLIP)
   - Attribution patterns learned on controlled data don't transfer to natural images
   STATUS: HIGH IMPACT - Fundamental limitation of current approaches

3. COMBINED MULTI-DIMENSIONAL UNDERSTANDING
   - All models fail significantly on combined understanding (spatial + numeric + attribute) (ScImage)
   - Individual dimension attribution performs well; joint attribution fails (ScImage)
   - Degradation increases combinatorially with more simultaneous dimensions (ScImage)
   STATUS: CRITICAL - Directly impacts information bottleneck design

4. NUMERIC AND QUANTITATIVE GROUNDING
   - Image models (DALLE, StableDiffusion) score <1.8/5 on numerical understanding (ScImage)
   - Counting and precise value representation fundamentally weak (ScImage)
   - Cannot reliably attribute text tokens referring to quantities (ScImage)
   STATUS: HIGH IMPACT - Limits scope of attributable phenomena

5. COMPLEX SCENE DISAMBIGUATION
   - Multiple similar objects cause incorrect attribution (ProtoDis-TBPS)
   - Background and contextual interference obscures target regions (ProtoDis-TBPS)
   - Attribution breaks down in cluttered, multi-object scenarios (ProtoDis-TBPS)
   STATUS: HIGH IMPACT - Affects real-world applicability

6. PHYSICS KNOWLEDGE AND CAUSAL UNDERSTANDING
   - Models lack understanding of physical laws (gravity, support, etc.) (ScImage)
   - Trajectory and spatial dynamics incorrectly attributed (ScImage)
   - Physics-dependent text descriptions cannot be attributed accurately (ScImage)
   STATUS: MEDIUM IMPACT - Affects scientific and technical domains

7. EVALUATION AND METRIC LIMITATIONS
   - Automated metrics have low correlation with human judgment (ScImage)
   - Standard multimodal metrics fail on spatial/directional attribution (ScImage)
   - Metrics like CLIPScore, BLIP achieve only 0.15-0.26 Kendall correlation (ScImage)
   - Human evaluation expensive and necessary ($3000/3k images)
   STATUS: MEDIUM IMPACT - Complicates validation of attribution quality

SPECIFIC VULNERABILITIES

• Noisy Captions: LAION-format captions cause 10% drop in retrieval performance (SynthCLIP)
• Compilation Failures: 28% of code generation attempts fail, masking true capability (ScImage)
• Format/Structure Errors: Valid attribution logic may fail due to output format issues (ScImage)
• Occlusion: Text referring to occluded regions cannot be attributed to visible areas (ProtoDis-TBPS)
• Computational Cost: Synthetic data generation requires 313 GPU-days per dataset (SynthCLIP)
• Copyright Issues: Training data may contain memorized content or copyrighted material (SynthCLIP)

QUANTITATIVE EVIDENCE

SynthCLIP Results:
- Synthetic vs. Real on ImageNet-Sketch: 11.9% vs. 22.9% (52% relative drop)
- Synthetic vs. Real on ImageNet-A: 7.42% vs. 8.32% (11% relative drop)
- Distribution shift persists even at 30M samples
- Finetuning on just 1M real samples recovers 4.4% performance gap

ScImage Results:
- Even best model (GPT-4O) scores <4.0/5 across all dimensions
- Combined dimension tasks: lowest scores
- Graph theory objects: average <1.7/5 (vs. >2.0 for other categories)
- Spatial understanding weakness leads to cascading failures
- Numeric understanding: DALLE/StableDiffusion <1.8/5 vs. >2.7 for attributes

ProtoDis-TBPS Results:
- Without semantic context decoupling: limited generalization in complex scenes
- With multiple pedestrians: disambiguation becomes a primary challenge
- Background interference reduces cross-modal matching robustness

KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR ADAPTIVE INFORMATION BOTTLENECK PAPER

1. Information bottleneck design must preserve MULTIPLE modalities simultaneously:
   - Cannot optimize for text-to-image alignment at expense of numeric grounding
   - Must balance spatial, semantic, and attribute information preservation

2. Attribution methods are fundamentally hampered by:
   - Lack of physics/world knowledge in base models
   - Difficulty with quantitative reasoning
   - Scene complexity and object disambiguation

3. Evaluation strategy should:
   - Include human judgment (automated metrics unreliable)
   - Test on combined understanding scenarios, not just individual dimensions
   - Evaluate robustness to noisy/misaligned training data
   - Measure generalization across synthetic-to-real domain shift

4. Practical concerns:
   - Computational efficiency critical for deployment
   - Output format reliability must be separately accounted for
   - Graceful degradation on difficult cases (physics, numerics, occlusion)

5. Architecture implications:
   - May need modality-specific bottlenecks for spatial vs. semantic information
   - Object-specific handling might be necessary for problematic categories
   - Contrastive learning alone insufficient; need explicit constraint satisfaction
