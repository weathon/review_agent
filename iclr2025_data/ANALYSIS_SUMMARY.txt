CATEGORY 2: FAIR COMPARISON (2 specific criticisms extracted)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Current Risk: HIGH
Your paper compares 3 methods without clear fairness specification.

Exact calibration example:
"[Methods] were trained on 8×A800 while [baseline] used 4×A100.
Wall-clock time comparisons are not provided on equal hardware."
(wd1 paper)

Impact on your paper:
- Were all methods (full FT, FNN-only, attention-only) tuned fairly?
- Did each get same LR search budget?
- Are parameter counts disclosed and equal?

Required fix: Clearly specify hyperparameter search procedure, parameter counts


CATEGORY 3: HYPERPARAMETER vs. CONTRIBUTION (2 specific criticisms extracted)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Current Risk: CRITICAL
Main finding might be "different LR for different components" = tuning, not contribution.

Exact calibration example:
"Without ablation, it is unclear which component drives the gains,
undermining the paper's core claim that both components are essential."
(DualRes paper)

Impact on your paper:
- Is the improvement from modularity or from better-tuned learning rates?
- Need: Fixed-LR ablation showing components differ even with same LR

Required fix: Show learning rate sensitivity curves + fixed-LR ablation


CATEGORY 4: MECHANISTIC UNDERSTANDING (2 specific criticisms extracted)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Current Risk: HIGH
Performance numbers alone insufficient; need to show *what* changed.

Exact calibration example:
"Without demonstrating that the learned properties are functionally important...
the interpretability claim remains correlational rather than causal."
(Dens3r/synthetic-to-real discussion)

Impact on your paper:
- Do attention weights actually change during attention-only tuning?
- Does attention-only affect FNN representations? (should be minimal)
- Functional evidence needed, not just performance improvements

Required fix: Add component update magnitude analysis, CCA analysis


CATEGORY 5: GENERALIZATION CLAIMS (2 specific criticisms extracted)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Current Risk: MEDIUM-HIGH

Exact calibration example:

Impact on your paper:
