TOP 7 WEAKNESSES FOR ACUPOINT DIFFUSION AUGMENTATION PAPER
==============================================================

1. NO EXPLICIT LANDMARK PRESERVATION CONSTRAINT
   Issue: Relies on implicit preservation; lacks physical constraints for acupoint locations
   Quote: "Due to the stochastic nature of diffusion algorithms... making it difficult to retain
           fine-grained details... Preserving image details and intrinsic properties thus requires
           careful design of training objectives and constraints." (IC-Light, p. 2)
   Fix: Add geometric loss, anchor-point masking, or optical flow consistency for landmarks

2. INADEQUATE QUALITY FILTERING FOR ANATOMICAL ACCURACY
   Issue: CLIP score filtering catches global failures but misses local anatomical distortions
   Quote: "CLIP scores [are] not to perform precise quality ranking, but rather to eliminate
           obviously mismatched samples... synthetic images sometimes fail to meet specific dataset
           requirements." (KnowData, p. 4-5)
   Fix: Multi-stage filtering with landmark detector validation and human review

3. UNCONTROLLED DATA DISTRIBUTION DIVERSITY
   Issue: No explicit control over facial pose/expression/ethnicity variation in augmented data
   Quote: "Without well-suited regularization and constraints, the model can easily degrade to
           random behaviors... arbitrary changes to the images, driven by dataset local minima."
           (IC-Light, p. 5)
   Fix: Conduct distribution analysis; control and validate synthesis parameters

4. MISSING PER-LANDMARK ACCURACY METRICS
   Issue: Standard metrics (accuracy, F1) don't measure whether acupoints are preserved
   Quote: "We conducted quantitative comparisons using metrics such as PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS...
           retains fine-grained image details and intrinsic properties." (IC-Light, p. 7-9)
   Fix: Report per-acupoint localization error, anatomical consistency, and landmark detection confidence

5. UNVALIDATED IP-ADAPTER + IC-LIGHT INTERACTION
   Issue: No evidence that combining IP-Adapter and IC-Light preserves anatomical landmarks
   Quote: "Existing studies employ various generation tricks but fail to effectively incorporate
           the relevant knowledge of the class itself into the generation process." (KnowData, p. 2-3)
   Fix: Comprehensive ablation studies on each component's impact on landmark preservation

6. NO GUIDANCE ON AUGMENTATION RATIO
   Issue: Optimal mix of synthetic to real data is unknown and remains an open challenge
   Quote: "Determining the optimal ratio for the three types of data, including the generated
           data, remains an open challenge." (CFDG, p. 10)
   Fix: Report experiments varying synthetic/real data ratios and provide optimization guidance

7. NO REALISTIC DOWNSTREAM CLINICAL VALIDATION
   Issue: Validation on synthetic tasks doesn't guarantee real clinical utility
   Quote: "We provide pretrained models to facilitate applications across diverse domains...
           handle more in-the-wild scenarios." (IC-Light, p. 2)
   Fix: Test on real clinical data; measure generalization across settings and practitioners
