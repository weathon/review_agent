🔴 CRITICAL WEAKNESSES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. ANATOMICAL INCONSISTENCIES
   Status: NOT VALIDATED
   Risk: Augmented acupoints could be placed at wrong facial locations
   Impact: Invalidates augmented training data for medical use
   Source: IC-Light paper - warns about structure preservation limitations
   
2. SYNTHETIC-TO-REAL GENERALIZATION GAP
   Status: NOT VALIDATED
   Risk: Augmented→Synthetic validation doesn't prove Real performance
   Impact: Landmark drift (5-8px) may be optimistic; real domain shift unknown
   Source: IC-Light, Sequential Disentanglement papers
   Action: Validate on real clinical acupoint images before claiming success

🟠 HIGH-SEVERITY WEAKNESSES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. LACKING ABLATION STUDIES
   Missing: IC-Light vs IP-Adapter contribution analysis
   Impact: Unclear which components are necessary
   Reference: Offline-to-Online RL paper emphasizes ablation importance

2. NARROW EVALUATION METRICS
   Using: CNN accuracy + MediaPipe landmarks (general, not clinical)
   Missing: Perceptual quality, clinical expert assessment, per-acupoint analysis
   Reference: Video Simulation paper shows multiple metrics needed

3. OUT-OF-DISTRIBUTION EVALUATION MISSING
   Tested on: Same facial dataset used for development
   Missing: Different skin tones, ages, ethnicities, lighting, devices
   Reference: Sequential Disentanglement emphasizes real-world protocol need

4. DOMAIN SHIFT NOT ADDRESSED
   Gap: Synthetic images vs real clinical facial images
   Issues: Different noise, lighting, quality, compression characteristics
   Reference: IC-Light paper explicitly addresses this challenge

5. SEMANTIC CONSISTENCY NOT VALIDATED
   Risk: Facial proportions distorted, bilateral symmetry violated
   Missing: Validation that acupoints stay anatomically valid
   Reference: KnowData paper shows synthetic images can have unrealistic content

6. NO HUMAN EVALUATION
   Using: Only automated metrics
   Missing: Clinical expert assessment of augmented image quality/realism
   Reference: Multimodal Simulation paper highlights human validation importance

7. INSUFFICIENT GENERALIZATION TO UNSEEN DATA
   Tested: Same distribution as training
   Missing: Zero-shot evaluation on external datasets/populations
   Reference: Sequential Disentanglement emphasizes zero-shot test importance

🟡 MEDIUM-HIGH SEVERITY WEAKNESSES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. PARAMETER TUNING WITHOUT JUSTIFICATION
   Parameters unexplained:
     - IC-Light guidance scales
     - IP-Adapter strength values
     - Number of augmentations per sample
   Impact: Cannot reproduce or verify optimality
   Reference: Learning on LoRAs shows importance of justified hyperparameters

2. MISSING BASELINE COMPARISONS
   Not compared with:
     - Traditional augmentation (rotation, scaling, elastic deformation)
     - Other structure-preserving methods
     - Medical imaging generative augmentation
   Impact: Complexity not justified relative to simpler alternatives

3. LIMITED ROBUSTNESS TESTING
   Not tested with:
     - JPEG compression (common in clinical practice)
     - Noise, blur, brightness changes
     - Real-world clinical workflow variations
   Reference: KnowData shows robustness essential for real systems

🟢 MEDIUM SEVERITY WEAKNESSES
