CRITICAL INSIGHTS FOR OOD GENERALIZATION PAPERS
================================================

1. EVALUATION DEPTH MATTERS MOST
   Papers with single benchmark: 3-5/10
   Papers with 3+ benchmarks: 6-8/10
   → Recommendation: 3-5 diverse rule-based reasoning benchmarks minimum

2. MECHANISTIC CLARITY IS ESSENTIAL
   Papers without explanation of "why": ≤5/10 even with good results
   Papers with clear mechanism: +2-3 point rating boost
   → Recommendation: Dedicate section to explaining how modules contribute to OOD

3. ABLATION STUDIES ARE NON-NEGOTIABLE
   Papers with weak ablations: Consistently cited as major weakness
   Papers with comprehensive ablations: Rated 1-2 points higher
   → Recommendation: Module selection, learning rates, layers, data efficiency

4. BASELINE FAIRNESS DIRECTLY IMPACTS CREDIBILITY
   Papers with unfair setups: Explicit criticism from reviewers
   Papers with fair comparisons: No criticism on baselines
   → Recommendation: Full FT, LoRA, BitFit with comparable budgets

5. OOD ANALYSIS EXPECTED FOR GENERALIZATION PAPERS
   Papers claiming generalization without OOD eval: Strong criticism
   Papers with explicit OOD protocol: Rated higher
   → Recommendation: New rules, distribution shifts, task composition
