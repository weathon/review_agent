CRITICAL QUESTIONS REVIEWERS WOULD ASK SCALERL
================================================================================

1. How can you be sure GRPO and DAPO were tuned fairly with equal compute budget?

2. Does the sigmoid scaling law generalize beyond math tasks?

3. Which recipe components (batch size, KL penalty, etc.) are statistically 
   significant? How do you know improvements aren't noise?

4. Why sigmoid over power laws? Did you test alternative functional forms?

5. What happens with extreme batch sizes or KL penalties?

6. Do the scaling curves hold for other model architectures?

7. How do inference costs (forward passes for rewards) affect the scaling law?

8. Are GRPO/DAPO truly the closest baseline comparisons? Any other scaling-focused 
   RL methods you should compare against?

9. Can the scaling law be applied to code generation, summarization, or dialogue tasks?

10. What explains any non-monotonic behaviors in the scaling curves?
