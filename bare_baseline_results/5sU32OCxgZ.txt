## Summary
This paper proposes TTVD (Test-Time adjustment by Voronoi Diagram guidance), a geometric framework for test-time adaptation (TTA) that re-interprets neighbor-based TTA methods through the lens of Voronoi Diagrams. Building on this connection, the authors extend from standard VD to Cluster-induced Voronoi Diagrams (CIVD) for integrating self-supervision with entropy minimization, and to Power Diagrams (PD) for improved noisy-sample filtering via boundary shifting. Experiments on CIFAR-10-C, CIFAR-100-C, ImageNet-C, and ImageNet-R show modest improvements over baselines benchmarked through the TTAB toolkit.

---

## Strengths
- **Geometric interpretability:** Framing TTA through Voronoi Diagrams provides an analytically tractable, visually interpretable view of the decision space. The visualizations in Figures 1–3 are illuminating and the entropy-landscape analysis in Figure 2 motivates the Power Diagram component in a clear way.
- **Incremental ablation:** Table 2 rigorously decomposes contributions from VD→CIVD→CIPD (28.4→22.7→20.5% error on CIFAR-10-C), making it easy to attribute which component drives performance.
- **Rigorous evaluation protocol:** Using the TTAB toolkit for fair, peer-reviewed comparison, and reporting both error and ECE (calibration), is commendable relative to ad-hoc comparisons common in TTA papers.
- **Robustness to data fraction for class means:** Table 4 shows stable performance even with only 1% of training data for Voronoi site computation, a practically useful result.
- **Adaptation dynamics:** Figure 4 demonstrates TTVD continues improving over many online batches while competitors stagnate, suggesting better long-term adaptation stability.

---

## Weaknesses

### Fatal
None.

### Major
- **Limited novelty of the VD↔TTA connection:** The observation that nearest-neighbor classifiers induce Voronoi tessellations is a decades-old, well-known fact in ML and computational geometry. Framing this as a "revealing insight" overstates the theoretical contribution. T3A and similar methods already implicitly exploit this structure; calling it a discovery is misleading.
- **Incomplete baseline comparisons:** The paper omits several important recent TTA methods: EATA (Niu et al., 2022 NeurIPS), CoTTA (Wang et al., 2022 CVPR), LAME (Boudiaf et al., 2022), TTT++ (Liu et al., 2021), and any 2024 methods. The comparison set skews toward older or weaker baselines, potentially inflating the perceived competitiveness of TTVD.
- **CIVD contribution reduces to multi-prototype ensemble:** The CIVD mechanism amounts to using 4 rotation-augmented copies as multiple prototypes per class, which is conceptually equivalent to standard multi-prototype or augmentation-ensemble approaches. The theoretical connection to CIVD adds formalism without providing additional design insight beyond what would be obvious from an augmentation perspective.
- **Marginal improvements on main benchmarks:** Error reductions of 0.7–1.6% over the best baseline are modest, especially when TTT achieves identical performance to TTVD on CIFAR-10-C under oracle settings (20.0%). Given the significant added complexity (CIVD + PD), the performance gain does not clearly justify the design.
- **Dependency on training data:** Computing class means requires access to a portion of training data (10% of ImageNet during pre-training), which violates the spirit of some TTA settings where source statistics are unavailable. This limitation is insufficiently discussed.

### Minor
- The influence function exponent γ=7 in Equation 4 is unmotivated; no ablation or sensitivity analysis is provided for this critical hyperparameter.
- The "diagram subtraction" noisy-sample filtering (Sec. 3.3) is described informally and lacks a precise algorithmic definition in the main text (deferred to Appendix H). The theoretical justification for why PD-boundary-shifted regions correspond to noisy samples is heuristic.
- The claim that CIVD "avoids negative transfer" by unifying objectives (p.6) lacks theoretical support and the empirical evidence provided is indirect.
- The footnote 1 disclaimer about oracle model selection reads as defensiveness rather than scientific clarity; it should be addressed directly in the experimental design.

### Tiny
- Grammar issues: "The first challenges is" (Introduction, §1), and minor stylistic inconsistencies.
- Self-citation (FedUD, Lei et al. 2025) in related work is borderline given ICLR anonymity requirements.
- The MNIST-C toy example (Figure 1B, 98.54%) is a highly favorable case and may mislead readers about the magnitude of real-world gains.

---

## Nice-to-Haves
- A theoretical analysis of why CIVD with rotation augmentation is particularly well-suited for image corruption robustness (vs. other augmentations like flips, crops).
- Comparison against methods that also compute training statistics (e.g., DUA, α-BN) to control for this advantage.
- Complexity and runtime analysis comparing TTVD to TENT/SAR/T3A.
- A study on continual/non-i.i.d. domain shifts (e.g., ImageNet-C with sequential corruptions) given the online adaptation context.

---

## Novel Insights
The paper's main novelty is connecting the well-known geometric identity (nearest-neighbor classification ≡ Voronoi tessellation) to TTA and using it as a scaffold to import two extensions (CIVD and PD) from computational geometry literature. The entropy-landscape analysis showing that standard entropy-threshold filtering misses noisy samples not near standard VD boundaries (Figure 2a) is a genuinely useful observation that motivates the PD component concretely. However, these insights are modest in scope — they reframe known results rather than offering fundamentally new theoretical understanding of why TTA fails or succeeds.

---

## Suggestions
- Add EATA, CoTTA, and at least two 2024 TTA baselines; the current baseline set is insufficient for a 2025 submission.
- Provide a proper ablation over the exponent γ in the influence function (Equation 4) and the temperature τ in Equation 3.
- Clarify the "diagram subtraction" noisy filtering algorithm (e.g., Algorithm box) with a precise definition of the region excluded.
- Discuss the assumption that class means can be computed from source training data and explore a variant where only BN statistics are used (consistent with source-free TTA).
- Tone down framing of the VD connection as a novel discovery; instead, position it as a principled unification that enables importation of richer geometric tools.
